A Tax Increment Financing (TIF) district is a special zone, with specific geographic boundaries, where some property tax revenue is set aside to fund local development projects instead of going to local governments like cities and school districts. However, the median Chicago homeowner’s tax bill increased by nearly $368 due to TIFs. This project modeled the individual cost of TIFs on addresses across the city.
