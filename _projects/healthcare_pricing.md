This project analyzes hospital price transparency data for the Chicagoland area, aiming to identify variations in payer-specific rates across hospitals. Using a curated dataset of 81 unique hospital price transparency files, we compare the median negotiated rates reported by the top five insurance companies—UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield (BCBS), Cigna, and Humana—across a set of 10 common procedures, identified using CPT codes.

By highlighting differences in pricing for the same procedures across hospitals and insurers, this analysis aims to support greater transparency, inform patient decision-making, and generate insights for policy and research on healthcare pricing variation in the region.
