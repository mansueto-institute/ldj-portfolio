Jails in Illinois are required to fill out a report everytime there is an unusual occurrence ranging from fires to restaint usage to suicide attempts. The Illinois Answers Project accumulated 28,000 PDFs of handwritten and typed documents which a Mansueto team turned into a machine-readable database using Optical Character Recognition (OCR) technology.
