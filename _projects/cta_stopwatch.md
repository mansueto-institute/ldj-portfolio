A novel dataset by Chi Hack Night showed the actual arrival time of each bus at every bus stop in Chicago from June 2022 to July 2024. We use this data as well as processed schedule data to assess the reliability and accessibility of bus service after the COVID-19 pandemic. The dashboard is still updated with current data.
