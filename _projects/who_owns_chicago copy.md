Who Owns Chicago? focuses on finding corporate ownership networks whose identities are often masked behind multiple entity names, such as LLCs or Trusts. Using hundreds of millions of rows of data from property tax records and LLC fillings, the project created novel algorithms for network analysis with a bottoms-up approach that captures thousands of smaller interconnected landlords.
